Upload Status lets you see the status of documents that you have uploaded to determine whether or not they have uploaded successfully.

 To View Upload Status
  1. In the Navigation Pad, click View Status. The Upload Status container displays recent upload status messages (if any are available). Your options are to delete the message or view more details about a particular message.
  2. To delete a message, check the message's checkbox and click the delete button.
  3. To view the details about a message, click that message's Report ID. More details are presented on Report Details window.
