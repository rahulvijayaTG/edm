Synchronize Data

The Synchronize Data screen helps you to manually initiate the flow of data from MDM to the Outbound Staging database.

 What you can do

Initiating the Outbound Staging Process

  1. In the Navigation Pad, click on Instances > Staging > Outbound Staging.
  2. Click on the Synchronize Data link under the Tasks heading.
  3. In the screen that is displayed, click on the Start Refresh button to initiate the synchronization process.
  4. To abort this process, at any point of time, click on the Cancel button.
