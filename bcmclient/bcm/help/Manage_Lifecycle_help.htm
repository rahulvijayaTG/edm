Managing Member Lifecycle

You can perform the delete, purge, and de-activate operations on the dimension member.

 

 What you can do

Managing Member Lifecycle

  1. In the Navigation Pad, you must have clicked on the Manage Lifecycle button to view the current screen.
  2. The Manage Member Lifecycle screen is displayed. 
  3. Select the dimension and level at which you want to perform the operation.
  4. Choose one of the operations. i.e., delete or purge or de-activate.
  5. Click on  Show Members button.
  6. Select one or more members from the list in the bottom. Click on the button with the operation name as its label. Changes are committed to database immediately.
