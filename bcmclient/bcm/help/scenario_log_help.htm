Scenario Logs

The Scenarios –Scenario Logs in the Scenarios screen is used to track all the changes that you have made to the parameters when working in a scenario.

You can also make notes of the above changes you made to specific parameters for each group edit. You can enter comments noting the changes made in the logs section. The Scenario Log stores these descriptions in the exact sequence, in which they are recorded. This logging of description enables you to understand the nature of changes that need to be applied to the system of records and business parameters, in order to realize the scenario.

After you have “Set Current Context”, the scenario chosen is displayed in the header. If you have picked any scenario other than Base Data, it would reflect in the header section of the screen along with your username. You can then click on the icon displayed to the left of the Refresh Icons to enter/view  the comments on the scenario in context.

  What you can do

 

Viewing Scenario Logs

  1. To view the Scenario Logs, click on the Scenario Logs icon located to left of the Refresh icons at the top-right corner of the content frame.

  2. The Scenario Logs screen is displayed. This screen provides Comments on the scenario, scenario creation date details, and the name of the person who created the Scenario.

  3. You can also perform a Scenario Log search for a more granular result. Enter the various search parameters in the Filters on the top of each column and click on Search.

  4. To add a Comment to a Scenario Log, enter the remarks in the Comments field, displayed below the Search button. The system will automatically fill the Creation Date and the Created By field for the newly added comment.

  5. Click Save to update the changes.
