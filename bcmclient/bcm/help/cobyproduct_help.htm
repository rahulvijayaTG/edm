CoBy Product Maintenance Page

A product created simultaneously with another product is a co-product, while a byproduct is created as a result of the creation of the product. In the Supply Path Detail area of the Supply Path page, click a supply chain and from the menu list, select the Add Co-By Products option.

The Co-By Products Maintenance page is displayed.

 

 What you can do

Adding a Co-By Product

To add a Co-By Product,
  1. In the Co-By Product Maintenance page, click the Add button. 
  2. The Item Location Search page is displayed. 
  3. Select the required Item Location and click the Select button. 
  4. The item is added in the Co-By Product list area of the Co-By Product Maintenance page.

Deleting a Co-By Product

To delete a Co-By Product,
  1. In the Co-By Product Maintenance page, select the Co-By product. 
  2. Click the Delete button. 
  3. You are prompted for confirmation. 
  4. Click the Yes button. 
  5. The selected co-by product is deleted.

Updating Co-By Product Values

To update Co-By Product values,
  1. In the Co-By Product Maintenance page, select the Co-By product.
  2. From the Quantity list, select the required operator and specify the required value. For e.g, select “= “from the Quantity list and enter a value of 10.
  3. In the By Product Flag field, enter either ‘Y’ or ‘N’.
  4. Click the Update button.
  5. The selected co-by product record is updated.
  6. To save the changes, click the Save button. Alternatively, click the Save and Return button to save the changes and return to the Supply Path page.
